*BODY*
Reality that bends, distorts, warps. No recognizable beginning or end, only a perpetual state of transformation. bow.distort.warp is a brief animation, yet within its brevity lives a paradox: the void as a space of endless possibility, the coexistence of nothing at everything. For 150 seconds the screen becomes a new space, where immaterial takes shape in a provisional and fleeting way, simultaneously representing  everything and nothing, presence and absence at once.


bow.distort.warp proposes a way of imagining reality not as something given, but as an open process; always in transit, always between states, never fully defined.
