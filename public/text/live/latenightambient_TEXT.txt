co_de_sus’s entry on #Aire Libre’s# Late Night Ambient series, in which the artist sets out on an exploration of synergy. The audience is invited to experience an immersive audiovisual set, combining both the artist's original tracks and ambient pieces that shaped his persona. The set presents a journey into the void, an hour of free fall between abstract and infinite landscapes. It is an immersive set that combines music and visuals to create a kind of synergy on your screen.
