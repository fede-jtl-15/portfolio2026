*BODY*
As a resident at Reflected Radio I have explored how to combine visuals with sound. Working with generative, procedural, and modular systems, I shape visuals where sound and image evolve together. Following the driving force of the music into abstraction and synchronicity, letting rhythm guide structure, motion, and form. The result is a focused, immersive dialogue between visuals and sound. For each performance I develop a set of rules and structures where the music, the algorithms and my sensibilities during the live set can coexist and evolve like living systems.
