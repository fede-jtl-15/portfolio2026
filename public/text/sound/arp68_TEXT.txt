Archive: Resonance Parallels In the late 60’s, student movements erupted in Mexico City and West Berlin, driven by a shared demand for justice, freedom, and change. Though shaped by different context, both became powerful testaments to the revolutionary force of youth and nonconformity. At the time, their voices were met with violence through police brutality and authoritarian injustice.


‘Archive: Resonance Parallels ’ (‘ARP’) pays homage to the students who rose against injustice, amplifying their spirit of resistance, compassion, and empathy nearly six decades later. It reminds us that protest remains a battleground and state violence an enduring threat. But ideals endure, carrying defiance, memory, and the relentless hope for change.
