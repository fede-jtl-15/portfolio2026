*BODY*
Dialogues is a response to Lee Ufan’s work, focusing around the concept of relatum, in which different objects are connected to other objects, while also remaining independent. Having this in mind,  I developed two independent audiovisual scenes that react in turn to two different sound sources. While both can exist separately, they enrich and relate to one another when shown together.


Both videos have a generative ambient sound that responds to the other., both the sound and the videos are not to repeat themselves, but to evolve to each others input, engangin in everlasting, ever evolving conversation. A conversation that is meaningless and understandable for the human ear
