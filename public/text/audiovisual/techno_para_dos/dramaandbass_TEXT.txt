For #@Techno para dos’# release #Drama#, a small catalogue of dance music for robots that invokes nostalgia on waves of electronica. The album serves as exploration and experimentation of a dramatic period of the artist's life, and lastly as a way of overcoming all of this drama.


I developed a visual for the piece Drama & Bass, the artist's experimental take on Drum n’ Bass. In it he explores fast moving rhythms and intense emotions. The sound of the piece gives voice to an overload of emotions through different samples and sonic experimentation. The visual tries to reflect the turbulent mindset of an undisclosed entity, giving a voice to a feeling and an emotion, letting it express itself to overcome the grief of its life to start healing through this process.


The video serves as an exploration of emotion, not providing concrete answers, but cathartic moments that allow ourselves to grow. While everyone feels differently, the video provides a small window into a particular soul.
