*BODY*
co_de_sus’ debut EP is a little block of experiments, ideas, and explorations of the influence between the moving image and the sonic representation of it. The work creates an audiovisual bridge, exploring different scenarios in. The whole EP creates a sandbox where sonic experimentation and visual representation are allowed to exist, play together and merge, influencing each other.

Through modular synthesisers and algorithms, the music and visuals embrace generative elements, making each piece unique. The visuals react to the audio in real time and are influenced by the sonic landscapes, and vice versa. The visual exploration combines both computer-generated images as well as analog footage that is later digitally manipulated, while the sonic landscapes are made out of a mix of extensive sound design, sampling, field-recording and randomness.
