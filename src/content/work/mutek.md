For the 2020 edition of Mutek Mexico the festival presented a hybrid format collaboration alongside Japan. For it, Federico Torres and Isaac Soto presented a 30 minute long audiovisual live show showcasing an ever evolving audiovisual palette.

For the sonic part Isaac Soto developed a modular set which was accompanied by audio reactive visuals illustrating the modular synths' journey, diversity and composition. The journey both sonically as well as visually goes from one extreme to another, from fast-paced rhythms to contemplative pads shimmering on the viewer's perception. From desaturated to colourful explosions, from overcrowded mental spaces, to valleys of calmness and meditation.
