Belmar enlisted singer / songwriter and producer ELIANGEL to develop this two single EP. Together they wanted to put her talents in the forefront, producing these two tracks that would let her showcase her range. These singles serve as an introduction of her musical persona into the scene.

Together we developed a couple of music videos, creating a trippy colourful world where the singer was able to express herself freely. In them we can see ELIANGEL in a variety of scenarios. In each of them she has a fierce presence, which is accentuated with the simple but mind-bending effects and scenarios that develop in the background.

Both cover images on the other hand, show a representation of the driving force of the tracks: her voice and the way it perfectly fits and flows into the production, creating these otherworldly images that invite the audience to dig deeper into the scenery the two artists birthed together.
